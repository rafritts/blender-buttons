1. Start with a cube
2. Flatted the cube into a square, but hand thickness
3. Add three loopcuts, equally spaced, both along the x axis, and y axis, so the flatted cube a 4x4 grid. 
3.1. Verify with feel. 
4. Select the 4 faces that will become the fingers, all at once.
4.1. Press I twice, so the inset works on each individual square.
4.2. Bring the inset in "a little bit".
4.3. Use S-Y to even out all the selected inset faces so the sides of the inset squares are the same length. 
4.4. Verify with feel. 
5. Use G-X to grab those inset faces and move them out a bit
6. Use E-X on those same inset faces and extrude about the length of the original 4x4 block. These are now the fingers.
7. Choose which finger will be the pinky. Pick the finger tip (the same inset face as before) and grab it. G-X, reduce its size. 
8. Pick the middle tip, and extend it a little bit.
9. Ring and Index finger dont change length. 
9.1. Verify with feel.
10. Go to the underside of the hand, to where the palm is, and select the three faces along the index finger column, on the bottom. 
10.1. Press E to extrude out. The thumb should take shape *going down* the z axis. 
10.2. Grab the leading edge of the thumb, thats closest to the index finger, and slide it up and in much closer to the palm, but not flush.
11. Subdivide the hand at level 1.
11.1. Verify with feel.
