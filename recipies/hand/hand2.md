1. Start with a cube
2. Flatted the cube into a square, but hand thickness
3. Add three loopcuts, equally spaced, both along the x axis, and y axis, so the flatted cube a 4x4 grid. 
3.1. Verify with feel. 
4. Select the 4 faces that will become the fingers, all at once.
4.1. Press I twice, so the inset works on each individual square.
4.2. Bring the inset in "a little bit".
4.3. Use S-Y to even out all the selected inset faces so the sides of the inset squares are the same length. 
4.4. Verify with feel. 
5. Use G-X to grab those inset faces and move them out a bit
6. Use E-X on those same inset faces and extrude about the length of the original 4x4 block. These are now the fingers.
7. Choose which finger will be the pinky. Pick the finger tip (the same inset face as before) and grab it. G-X, reduce its size. 
8. Pick the middle tip, and extend it a little bit.
9. Ring and Index finger dont change length. 
10. Add two loopcuts to each finger, representing knuckle placement. 
10.1. Verify with feel.
11. Go to the underside of the hand, to where the palm is, and select the three faces along the index finger column, on the bottom. 
11.1. Press E to extrude out. The thumb should take shape *going down* the z axis. 
11.2. Grab the leading edge of the thumb, thats closest to the index finger, and slide it up and in much closer to the palm, but not flush.
12. Subdivide the hand at level 1.
12.1. Verify with feel. 
13. Select the mid-section outer edges of the thumb, should be three edges, in the middle of the thumb extrusion, facing out.
14. Use Alt+S to "puff out" the joint of the thumb. 
15. Select the two bottom edges of the thumb, and use G-Y to push more of the base of the thumb out in line with step 14.
15.1. The result from 14 and 15 should look somewhat organic. This is the BASE of the thumb, the fleshy part of the thumb that connects to the hand. 
16. Select the bottom two faces of the thumb. and rotate them 45 degrees down. 
17. Move the two faces you just rotated approximately one grid square closer to the index finger, on that same axis. 
18. Press E to extrude from these two faces to create the thumb digit itself. 
19. Shape the thumb into a slight taper. 
20. Grab the second to bottom *inside* face and press G to move it inward. This creates the fleshy part of the thumbpad. 
21. Select the edges running down the length of the thumb on the outside, and press ALT+S to puff them out a bit to give the thumb some shape.
21.1. Verify with feel. 
22. Grab the single vertex on the top of the hand, in-between the ring and middle finger.
22.1. Use proportional editing. Press G-X and move all the finger geometry across the knuckles, by dragging this one vertex. Move it a little. 
22.2. Repeat the same movement on the underside, with the corrosponding single vertex. 
23. Repeat this process for the other two single point vertex between the pink and ring finger, and index and middle finger, but dont move the vertex as far. 
23.1. The result should be a slight curve of the webbing between the fingers. 
23.2. Verify with feel.
24. Select the top edges running down the top of the hand. "the spine". 
24.1. Use proportional editing. Press G-Z and pull the top of the hand up a little bit. 
24.2. Repeat the same movement for the underside of the hand, to give it a gentle arch. 
24.3. Verify with feel.
25. Select the bottom edge, of each fingertip. Press G-G to edge slide these finger tip edges *inwards* towards the fingers, a little bit. Approx a 30 degree angle.
25.1 Repeat the same edge slide, for the next inner digit. Approx 15 degree angle. 
26. Done. Verify with feel. 
27. Shade smooth.
